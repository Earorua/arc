Design chosen: preserve the fixed-target read collector and its 45-second child limit, add at most two retries (three attempts total) only for an explicitly classified transport failure of one existing read operation. Wait 1 second between attempts. Repeated reads are allowed only after the prior child emits close with its inherited streams closed. Unknown failures, output overflow, projection/type/identity rejection and unconfirmed shutdown must not retry. No CLI command permitting a mutation may enter this helper. Export is the existing read-only init/from-dash/no-delegate-c3 operation, uses a fresh neutral temporary directory per attempt, and retains every failed directory path. Source/config acceptance gates remain unchanged.

Every read's attempt metadata preserves start/end, exit code/signal, timeout, child-close confirmation, and stdout/stderr hashes/byte lengths; never persist arbitrary raw output, headers, tokens, bodies or environment values. A final success includes earlier failures and is still part of one complete, fresh collection. Do not merge records from prior collections. Start/end deployment identities still bracket all reads. Keep exact eight SELECTs, zero-write metadata and all version/config/source/lifecycle/Workflow/Secret-type checks. Maximum collector wall budget is 12 minutes; once exceeded, stop subsequent cloud reads, preserve a failed report, and do not extend the existing 15-minute gate freshness window.
